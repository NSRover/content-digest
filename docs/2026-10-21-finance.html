Where Is The Crypto Alpha Hiding This Cycle?
gemini-flash (gemini-2.5-flash)fallback
TLDR

The current crypto market, while still early in a bull cycle, is characterized by significant dispersion, rewarding active managers who can identify strong fundamentals. The intersection of crypto and AI is showing promising, legitimate use cases, particularly in areas like financing, privacy, and decentralized coordination for AI agents. Investors are finding alpha by focusing on high-conviction assets with strong fundamentals, even if they are consensus plays, and by being comfortable with volatility and longer time horizons.

Key points
  • The intersection of crypto and AI is moving beyond forced narratives, with legitimate projects emerging in areas like open-source AI agent frameworks, privacy-focused inference routing, and financing for AI infrastructure (e.g., GPU clusters).
  • Crypto's strengths in capital formation and permissionless, global coordination make it suitable for financing AI infrastructure and enabling machine-to-machine transactions for AI agents, potentially at lower costs than centralized systems.
  • Privacy and encryption by default are key differentiators for crypto AI projects compared to traditional AI, which often prioritizes development over privacy concerns.
  • The current market environment offers significant dispersion, allowing active managers to generate alpha by identifying fundamentally strong projects, a shift from previous cycles where almost all assets rose together.
  • While Bitcoin remains the primary benchmark for most LPs, funds are increasingly focused on outperforming it and generating absolute returns, with many liquid crypto funds showing strong performance year-to-date.
  • Sizing into illiquid tokens requires a long-term view and comfort with volatility, with funds budgeting for a 5-10% variance in entry and exit prices, as crowding can lead to rapid price movements.
  • In venture investing, while seed stage still offers significant opportunities for high-quality founders and rapid returns (50x in 1-2 years), the capital intensity of early-stage crypto projects may be decreasing due to AI's impact on operational efficiency.
