A Once in a Lifetime Economic Reset Is Arriving.

TLDR

The US economy is experiencing a historic divergence where consumer sentiment is at record lows while stock market optimism is at record highs. This gap is driven by a structural shift favoring corporate profits and capital over labor, creating an unsustainable environment that may eventually require a painful economic reset.

Key points
  • There is a significant disconnect between the financial economy and the real economy, as the S&P 500 has tripled in inflation-adjusted value over 15 years while real personal income growth has stalled and recently begun to decline.
  • Corporate profits as a percentage of GDP have reached record highs of nearly 12%, while wages as a share of GDP have dropped to their lowest levels since 1948, indicating that corporations are capturing an increasing share of economic output.
  • Tax policy has systematically shifted over the last four decades to favor corporations over consumers, with the average American facing higher effective tax rates while corporate tax rates have declined to their lowest levels since the 1980s.
  • Historical precedents, such as the period between 1965 and 1980, suggest that a rebalancing of the economy to favor the consumer often results in a violent repricing of financial assets and a significant decline in equity values.
  • Because current policy continues to favor capital over labor, owning productive assets remains the most effective strategy for wealth building, though investors must remain vigilant for signs that the policy cycle is shifting.
