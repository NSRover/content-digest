TLDR

The US budget deficit expanded to a single-month record of $404 billion in October, nearing the entire $439 billion annual deficit recorded in 2015. Concurrent with this expanding deficit, 10-year US Treasury yields spiked back above 5%, signaling an ongoing US bond bear market and a worsening fiscal debt spiral. The guest argues that higher interest rates and bond yield spikes are fundamentally bullish for gold because they reflect persistent inflation and severe systemic risks.

  • The US budget deficit for October reached $404 billion, driven by skyrocketing debt interest, defense spending, and entitlement outlays.

  • Drawing from *A Pocketbook of Gold*, co-authored with Jim Sinclair, the guest argues that higher market interest rates support gold because central banks raise rates to catch up with persistent inflation.
