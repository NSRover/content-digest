Key points
  • Ray Dalio's argument for a US debt crisis is based on the government's large deficits, increasing interest expenses, and the need to refinance trillions in debt, which he believes will lead to investors demanding higher rates or the central bank resorting to money printing.

  • The market continues to distinguish between the US government's fiscal challenges and the essential role of Treasury securities for liquidity, collateral, and protection against macroeconomic risks, especially in a "K-shaped" economy where demand for safety remains high.
