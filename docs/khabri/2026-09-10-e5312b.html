Deep Dive — What A Loan Is Worth Until Someone Asks

Blue Owl has just written its most senior loans to a packaging firm called Loparex down from a hundred cents on the dollar to almost nothing, inside a few months. Eurodollar University's point is not the one borrower but the mechanism: private credit does not trade on an exchange, so what a loan is worth is whatever the manager's own model says — and the manager is paid on that number. The quiet is not safety, it is the absence of anyone asking. Public investors have noticed, pricing the listed lenders well under their stated book. Meanwhile the hyperscalers' AI borrowing is crowding ordinary companies out of the dollar market and into Europe. — Eurodollar University

De Kleine Khabri — Voor de aanstaande ouders · Week 33

Week thirty-three, and forty-nine days left to run — seven weeks exactly. Little changes in shape from here; it is weight, and lungs rehearsing. The next midwife appointment is on the 22nd.

Elders — two threads this week, both about expectations rather than technique. The best-rated post of the month in r/NewParents is a parent writing that giving up every attempt to hack the baby's sleep is what finally brought calm; the replies keep returning to the same thing — you can do it all right and have a bad night, all wrong and have a good one. A second, widely-read thread asks bluntly at what week the newborn days stop feeling like one long repeat of the same day. The answers scatter, and the scatter is the answer: some say eight weeks, many say three months, and a fair number admit that at nine or twelve months it still feels that way — it just stops being a surprise. Parents talking, not guidance. r/NewParents · geverifieerd 7 sep
