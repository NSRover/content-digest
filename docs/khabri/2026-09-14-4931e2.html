Coronaprik-najaarsronde (this autumn’s Covid vaccination round)

▸ Do not book a GGD slot on the strength of the pregnancy — this year it is not a ground.RIVM opens the round on 21 September and takes appointments until 27 November, but the groups it lists are 70 and over, 50–69 with a medical condition, medical high-risk groups, and care staff with direct patient contact. Pregnancy is not among them: the Gezondheidsraad judges the added value for all pregnant women too limited now that the risk of severe illness and of pre-term birth has fallen back from its pandemic level. An underlying condition still counts on its own footing — put that to the verloskundige rather than judging it at home. Deep Dive — The Plumbing, Not The Announcement

maneco64’s point about de-dollarisation is that the interesting part is not the summit communiqués but the pipes already laid. India’s UPI carries 550 million users and Brazil’s Pix 170 million; between them the two domestic instant-payment systems have moved more than $10 trillion in eighteen months, account to account, with no Visa, Mastercard or Swift anywhere in the path. Both are now talking to overseas central banks about linking up. The Financial Times’ observation, which he leans on, is that such a network only becomes a real alternative once there is a common settlement asset behind it — national currencies, a basket, or gold. Nothing here is imminent. But the rupee leg of a household that sends money to India is a fair thing to watch, and it is being rebuilt quietly rather than announced. — maneco64
