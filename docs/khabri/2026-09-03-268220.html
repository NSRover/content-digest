The First Daycare Offer Arrives

Five aanmeldingen went out at the end of August. The first answer came back yesterday afternoon, and it is a yes: Feline, of CompaNanny’s planning and placement team, has been through the schedule at the Baarsjes location and can offer kindje Agarwal a place — Monday and Wednesday, starting 3 May 2027. A non-binding contract follows in a separate mail, and the signature request that comes with it stays open for two weeks; changing the days, or declining, is done through that same request rather than by letter. The reply went back the same evening: the preference as originally stated was Monday and Tuesday, and they have asked whether that is still possible. CompaNanny answers within three working days, which lands just before Monday’s rondleiding at Cornelis Dirkszstraat. Four other providers have still not answered.

Family Desk — ’t Lieveheersbeestje answered too, and helpfully: no fresh aanmelding is needed, they will simply move the start date on the existing one. The plan went back — Monday and Tuesday from 3 May, four days from late October.

De Kleine Khabri — Voor de aanstaande ouders · Week 32

Week thirty-two, day two, and fifty-six still to run. Somewhere near 1.9 kilos and forty-three centimetres. The lungs remain the long job; most of what is added from here is weight rather than length. Elders — the most-read new-parent thread of the month, over five hundred replies, asks whether newborn tired is worse than third-trimester tired. Strikingly, it does not settle: one camp says no night ever catches you up, an equally large one says the pregnancy fog and the heartburn left at the birth, and broken sleep with an end in sight was easier than lying awake without. The one measure both camps keep returning to is dividing the nights in advance, so one of you sleeps a whole block. r/NewParents
· And a quietly practical one: what to do with a leaked-through romper.
