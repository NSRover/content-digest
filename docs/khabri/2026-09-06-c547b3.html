The Leave Letter Is Due Thursday

Zwangerschapsverlof (maternity leave) is not something an employer arranges — the employee has to ask for it, and Rijksoverheid puts a hard floor under the request: at least three weeks before the leave starts, and never later than seven weeks before the uitgerekende datum. With 29 October as the due date, that floor falls on Thursday. Two steps, both this week. Ask the verloskundige for a zwangerschapsverklaring — Tuesday’s four o’clock appointment is the natural moment, and it is that document which states the vermoedelijke bevallingsdatum. Then give it to the employer in writing with the chosen start date, and ask for confirmation in writing back. Only after that can the employer request the WAZO-uitkering from UWV, so a late letter holds up the money as well as the leave. — rijksoverheid.nl · geverifieerd 15 aug

Deep Dive — The Couple As A Survival Team

Stan Tatkin, a couples therapist, tells Chris Williamson that love is not what keeps a partnership working — design is. Under stress the brain moves resources from the executive areas to fight-or-flight, which biologically narrows how much of the other person’s interest anyone can hold in mind; a cooperative relationship quietly becomes a solo sport at exactly the moment it should not. His remedy is procedural rather than romantic: treat yourselves as a survival team, become expert in each other’s vulnerabilities, and write the agreements down instead of implying them. The one rule he will not soften is repair — a rupture mended within about thirty minutes, to the other partner’s satisfaction, before it hardens into resentment. Face to face, he adds: text is where repair goes wrong. — Chris Williamson, with Dr Stan Tatkin
