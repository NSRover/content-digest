No Ghee in the Suitcase for Kraambezoek

When a baby is born, food often comes from India: homemade ghee for the new mother, panjiri and laddoos made with ghee, peda and barfi. The NVWA (the Dutch food and product safety authority) is plain about it: from outside the EU you may bring no meat, milk or dairy — the allowance is zero kilos, and what customs finds on arrival is taken away. Ghee is clarified butter, so it counts as dairy; so do paneer and khoya. The exceptions are sealed, branded baby formula and baby food up to 2 kg, and sweets where the dairy is “no longer recognisable”, such as chocolate and biscuits. Whether milk sweets like peda count is not said, so assume they do not. Why now: the calendar has KL 872 from Delhi on Thursday 22 October, and Mukesh and Nisha’s Dutch visas are in process. Tell anyone travelling before they pack. Ghee and paneer are easy to buy here. Fish up to 20 kg and honey up to 2 kg are allowed. — nvwa.nl, verified 7 Oct

Rookmelder op elke slaapverdieping (smoke alarms are a legal must)

Many newcomers don’t know that a smoke alarm in the Netherlands is a legal requirement, not just advice. Every floor with a living room, bedroom or kitchen must have one. An attic or cellar used only for storage or laundry is exempt. If the nursery is on a floor where nobody sleeps yet, that floor now needs one. The owner must fit them; if you rent, the landlord must.▸ Before 29 October, check every floor where someone will sleep, including the nursery’s. If you rent, report a missing alarm to the landlord in writing. If you own, fit one yourself: on the ceiling, at least 50 cm from a wall or corner and 30 cm from a lamp, and choose a type that lasts at least 10 years. Press the test button now and then.act by: the due date, 29 Oct — rijksoverheid.nl, verified 7 Oct

Thirty-six weeks and six days, 22 days to the due date. Tomorrow the baby reaches 37 weeks and counts as full-term, so a birth any day from now is normal. The lungs and brain keep maturing, and the baby goes on gaining weight. Friday’s midwife visit is the place to ask what to bring, and when to call. An expectant mother due mid-November asked whether her partner should cancel a multi-day trip six hours away at the end of October.
