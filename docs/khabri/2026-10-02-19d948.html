Holiday After the Leave? Ask Now, in Writing

With the baby due in 27 days, the weeks straight after the leave are worth fixing now rather than in the last week at home. Rijksoverheid is plain about the rule: an employer must in principle agree to a holiday request, may object only for serious business reasons or a company-wide shutdown, and must do so in writing within two weeks of receiving it. The Civil Code (art. 7:638) adds that without a timely written objection the holiday stands as asked. So: work out when Raj’s bevallingsverlof ends — at least ten weeks after the birth, plus any unused pregnancy leave — and when Nirbhay’s birth leave ends; pick the weeks to add; email each employer a dated request and keep the sent mail. A second reason not to wait: statutory days built up in 2026, including during leave, lapse on 1 July 2027. Above-statutory days and CAO terms can differ. — rijksoverheid.nl, verified 2 Oct

Nabestaandenpensioen na een baanwissel (survivor pension stops after leaving a job)

▸ Under the new pension rules, cover for a partner and children under 25 runs only 3 or 6 months after leaving a job, then stops unless you opt for voluntary continuation with the old fund. Check now, per parent, how your current scheme covers partner and child; at any job exit, read the fund’s letter at once and decide before that period ends.applies at every job change; new rules apply by 1 Jan 2028 at the latest, many funds sooner — rijksoverheid.nl, verified 30 Sep

Deep Dive — A winter of small, steady effort

Bodybuilder and YouTuber Sam Sulek tells Chris Williamson that consistent, hard effort on the basics beats chasing clever, niche techniques — progress comes from turning up, not from optimising. He frames the dark months as a “winter arc”: a quieter, more inward season in which you choose to act even without motivation and let momentum build. To climb out of a slump or start a habit, his advice is to begin with one small step and repeat it; seeing someone else manage the thing helps prove it can be done. And he puts his marriage above his career: close relationships rest on who you are, not what you achieve, and that is what lasts. Why it matters to us: a winter with a newborn will be all small, repeated steps. One athlete’s view, not research. — Chris Williamson, “How To Go Hard, Even When You Don’t Want To”, content digest
