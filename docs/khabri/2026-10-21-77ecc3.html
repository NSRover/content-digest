The Baby Bath Has Arrived: Four Rules First

Babypark’s three DPD parcels landed yesterday afternoon: the thermobad, its stand and a bath seat. Before the first bath, VeiligheidNL’s “De 4 van veilig in bad” is worth reading together. Stay with the baby the whole time, “not even for a moment” away — a child can drown in 10 cm of water. Let nothing distract you: not the phone, not the doorbell. No badzitje (bath seat) — VeiligheidNL says it gives a false sense of safety, so the new seat is one to check against that advice. And the water at 37°: its blog calls 36–38° safe and suggests testing with an elbow and a bath thermometer with readable numbers, not a colour strip — worth having out before the baby comes. The kraamverzorgende usually does the first bath with you. Informational, not medical advice. — kinderveiligheid.nl, verified 21 Oct

Family Desk — Geen kettinkje om (no necklaces or nazar bracelets on the baby). Black threads, nazar beads and gold chains are classic newborn gifts. The US FDA reports babies strangled or choked by necklaces and bracelets, including a toddler who died during a nap. Nothing round the neck; bracelets only briefly, watched, never in bed. Tell visiting family before the kraamvisite. — fda.gov, verified 21 Oct

Kraamtranen of depressie (baby blues versus postnatal depression)

▸ For both of you to know: per Thuisarts, crying and low moods in the first days are normal and usually pass after about 10 days. Low mood lasting more than 2 weeks — no joy in the baby, constant worry about not coping, anxiety — may be depression after the birth, which is “good to treat”: book the GP. Call straight away (midwife in the first 10 days, then GP) if the mother becomes very anxious, confused or unable to care for the baby. The partner often notices first. Informational, not a diagnosis.after the birth (due 29 Oct) — thuisarts.nl, verified 21 Oct

Navelstompje (the umbilical stump)

▸ It usually comes away by itself after about a week, and a little bleeding for a few days after is normal; you need do nothing to it. If dirty, wash with water and soap; don’t fold the nappy over it. See the GP if the navel gets thicker or the skin around it warm — on darker skin redness may not show, so watch for swelling and warmth. With fever as well: call the GP at once.after the birth — thuisarts.nl, verified 21 Oct

Veilig dragen (safe babywearing in a sling or carrier)

▸ If you plan to carry the baby: nose and mouth free and the face always in view; not too warm, no closed coat over the baby; back and neck supported in an upright M-position, high enough to kiss the head — never chin-on-chest, where breathing suffers. Lay the baby flat regularly. Extra care until about 4 months, especially asleep. Not while cycling, driving or cooking, and not as a regular sleeping place.from birth — kinderveiligheid.nl, verified 21 Oct
