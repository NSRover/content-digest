Allowances: Check Your Savings Before 8 December

The tax office’s allowances service has put a hard date on next year’s lower savings limit. From 1 January 2027 a household with a toeslagpartner loses zorgtoeslag (health-care allowance) and kindgebonden budget (the income-tested child allowance) once joint savings and investments top €158,748 — down from €184,633. Anyone above that on 1 January must say so in Mijn toeslagen by 8 December; reporting opens 1 November. Miss it and the allowance is paid back. Why it matters to us: the baby makes kindgebonden budget a live question for 2027, and parents with a child together count as toeslagpartners even unmarried, so both your assets are added up. A warning letter goes out late November to likely cases, but no letter does not mean you are safe; from 24 November Mijn toeslagen shows whether you still qualify. — belastingdienst.nl (Dienst Toeslagen, 15 Oct), verified 23 Oct
