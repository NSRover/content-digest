Deep Dive — Gold Is Money Again

maneco64 reads a UBS chief strategist’s line — that gold is money again — as the moment a reversal finished. In 2004 the Financial Times called gold a barbaric relic bound for the way of the dodo. The measure it had been judged by, gold against real yields, broke in February 2022, when Western governments froze $630 billion of Russia’s reserves and every reserve manager learned that sovereign debt can become unavailable overnight. Developing-country central banks have since taken gold from five per cent of reserves to eleven, still short of the twenty-six developed markets hold. Closer to home, De Nederlandsche Bank is bringing a large part of its own gold back from New York and Ottawa — some to London, some to a military base in Zeist. — maneco64
