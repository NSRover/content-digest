Noon Today, The Only Offer On Paper

CompaNanny sent the reminder yesterday morning: the rondleiding is today at twelve, on Cornelis Dirkszstraat, with a number to ring if anything changes. It is more than a look round a building. This is the one crèche that has answered with a date rather than a list — Monday and Wednesday from 3 May 2027 — and the ondertekenverzoek that followed it can be signed digitally for roughly another eleven days. The Tuesday the household asked for does not exist there; kindje Agarwal stays on the waiting list for it whichever way this goes. So the questions worth taking at noon are the ones a signature makes permanent: how long that Tuesday wait really runs, whether a Wednesday can be swapped later, and what a Monday morning in the group looks like. Their own reminder points to a checklist on the site for what to watch for on a rondleiding: worth ten minutes beforehand, because this visit is the last unstructured look at the place before a signature makes it the arrangement. — post & agenda

Donorregister na drie jaar in Nederland (organ donation: silence becomes consent)

▸ Register your own choice now, both of you, at donorregister.nl with DigiD — rather than waiting for the letter to arrive.Rijksoverheid names newcomers alongside eighteen-year-olds: people who come to live here and register with a gemeente are asked, after three years, to enter a choice. The clock runs from the inschrijving, separately for each partner — not from a birthday or a nationality. Leave the letter and a reminder follows; leave that one too and you are registered as “geen bezwaar”, which means organs and tissue may be donated. There are four options, one of which hands the decision to a partner rather than to a default, and a registered choice can be changed as often as you like — so nothing is lost by doing it early. From late October the post here fills with birth letters from gemeente, SVB and zorgverzekeraar, and this is precisely the envelope that ends up on that pile. altijd open — de reactietermijn per brief staat op de brief zelf — rijksoverheid.nl · donorregister.nl · geverifieerd 7 sep

Deep Dive — The Dollar’s Rivals Keep Not Arriving

Brent Johnson finds that seventeen years of BRICS summits have built almost nothing. Of sixteen initiatives announced since 2009, one institution functions — the New Development Bank — and some sixty-five per cent of its loans are approved in dollars. The communiqués barely move: of ninety-one words in the core paragraphs of the 2024 and 2025 declarations, eighty-nine were identical. And central bankers in India, Brazil and South Africa have said on the record that they are not pursuing de-dollarisation. — Brent Johnson, Milkshakes Pod
