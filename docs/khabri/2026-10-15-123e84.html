Grandparents’ Visas: Count the Days Now

The grandparents’ visas are in, and our leave plan counts on their help until about 8 January. Before KL 872 from Delhi lands next Thursday, check that date really fits. Each visa sticker carries two limits, and both bind: “Until”, the last day to leave the Schengen area, and “Duration of stay”, the number of days allowed — at most 90 in any 180, and a visa may be shorter. Note both for each grandparent, count from the real arrival day (an earlier Schengen trip within 180 days counts too), and book the return on or before the earlier limit. Since April the border registers every entry and exit electronically, so an overstay is logged automatically; more than three days can bring a one-year ban across the EU, and an extension is only for unexpected emergencies. — netherlandsworldwide.nl, ind.nl, verified 15 Oct

De Adviseur

Autostoeltje (a car seat for the ride home)

▸ If the baby goes home by car — ours or anyone’s — have an approved seat ready before 29 Oct and know how it fits. Children under 1.35 m need one, front or back. Buying new: choose UN R129 (“i-Size”); an R44/03 or R44/04 seat is still allowed. Front seat only with the airbag switched off; rear-facing as long as possible; no belt extender. A taxi is exempt for under-3s. Before the due date, 29 Oct rijksoverheid.nl · verified 15 Oct

Terugroepactie Zeeman (Zeeman baby-clothes safety recall)

▸ Bought baby clothes at Zeeman this summer? Check the care label. A baby sleeping bag, art. 78021-123445, sold 16 Jun–25 Sep: the zip joint can loosen and the baby may slip down inside. A romper, art. 75433 (122691-4 / -11), sold 15 Jun–20 Aug: buttons can come off. Stop using either and return it to a store — replaced or refunded, receipt not needed. Now; recall listed to 15 Dec zeeman.com · verified 15 Oct

Eerste vaccinaties (the baby’s first jabs)

▸ RIVM posts the vaccination invitation when the baby is about 4 weeks old; if nothing has come by 2 months, contact RIVM’s regional office. The first consultatiebureau appointment is at 6–9 weeks: rotavirus drops, plus a combined jab only if no whooping-cough jab was given in pregnancy. Born near 29 Oct, that lands roughly 10–31 Dec (our sum) — worth knowing before holiday travel plans. The RSV jab in week one is separate. After the birth; first visit ~mid-December rijksvaccinatieprogramma.nl · verified 15 Oct
