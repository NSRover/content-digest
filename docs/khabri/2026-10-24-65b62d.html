Brug Overtoomsesluis dicht (Overtoom bridge closed to cars and tram 1)

▸ Until 1 May 2027 cars and taxis cannot cross between Surinameplein and the Overtoom; traffic detours via the Zeilbrug and Kinkerbrug, up to 15 minutes extra, and tram 1 runs another route. Drive the route to the birth hospital once with the detour in it, and add the time when calling a taxi or planning kraamvisite. Bikes and pedestrians can usually cross (one side shut to bikes until end February); at night it may close to everyone.now – 1 May 2027 · on our doorstep — amsterdam.nl, verified 24 Oct

Geel worden in de eerste week (newborn jaundice: when to call)

▸ Yellowing on day 3–4 is common and usually gone in a week; feed about 10 times a day. Call the midwife, GP or out-of-hours post at once if the baby turns yellow in the first 24 hours, or if yellow comes with poor drinking, drowsiness, under 36° or from 38°, a high-pitched cry, or pauses in breathing. Builds on the 18 Oct note: on a darker skin, check the whites of the eyes.from the birth, first week — thuisarts.nl, verified 20 Oct

Denk Vooruit noodpakket (a 72-hour emergency kit, baby included)

▸ The government’s list: 3 litres of water per person per day, long-life food, battery radio and charged power bank, torch, first aid, blankets, whistle, cash, ID copies, numbers, spare keys — plus baby food and medicines (nappies are the correspondent’s own addition). Agree who collects the baby if one of you cannot get home. Low urgency: best done once the first weeks settle.any time · check every six months — denkvooruit.nl, verified 24 Oct

De Kleine Khabri — Week 40

Thirty-nine weeks and two days; five days to the due date. The baby is fully formed and mostly putting on fat, which keeps it warm after birth. Only a small share of babies arrive on the date itself: anything from now to about two weeks past it is normal. The midwife on Tuesday is the next check; past 41 weeks the talk usually turns to options for a nudge.
