Week Thirty-Two Opens In A Scanning Room

Week thirty-two begins today, and it begins with an appointment: eleven o’clock, the tweede groeiecho with the verloskundige. Deep Dive — What Jackson Hole Left Out

Eurodollar University’s reading of the Jackson Hole meeting is that the hawkish story told there is not the one the data tells. Fed officials take the official unemployment rate at face value and conclude the labour market can carry higher rates; the JOLTS figures show hiring below the two per cent line, the level at which people historically stop looking and quietly leave the count altogether. Treasury’s Scott Bessent argues the current price pressure is a supply shock, not demand — European core inflation decelerated to 2.4 per cent even as the headline jumped. The bond market appears to agree: break-even rates are not pricing a 1970s repeat, and the drift up in long yields looks like curve mechanics rather than anyone refusing to hold Treasuries. — Eurodollar University, ‘WTF Just Happened at Jackson Hole?’

De Kleine Khabri — Voor de aanstaande ouders · Week 32

Week thirty-two, day one, and fifty-seven still to run. Somewhere near 1.9 kilos and forty-three centimetres. The lungs remain the long job, surfactant building week by week; most of what is added from here is weight rather than length. Elders — the most-answered newborn thread of the month, nearly four hundred replies, asks why contact naps carry a stigma. The camps split hard — some say it was the only way their baby slept and it changed nothing later, others that a baby who only sleeps on its mother mostly means a mother who does not sleep. The most-agreed reply is a third one: that this is temperament rather than parenting, and not a thing to decide in advance. r/NewParents
· And a quieter thread: what parents actually did three days after the birth. The answers are strikingly small — bed, then bedroom, then house — and several say they felt fine, did too much, and paid for it. Dutch practice differs, though; here the kraamzorg is in the house with its own instructions. r/beyondthebump
