Gifts From India: Know the Customs Limits First

A flight from Delhi is on the calendar for Thursday 22 October, and gifts from India tend to arrive around a birth — packed or posted. Dutch customs sets three limits. A parcel is free of duty and VAT only up to €45, sent once and for personal use; above that VAT and duty are due, plus the carrier’s handling fee. In a suitcase from outside the EU, goods up to €430 per traveller are exempt — and gold jewellery, the traditional bangles or chain for a baby, counts as goods. Cash or investment gold of €10,000 or more must be declared on arrival, per person, including money carried for others; not declaring risks a fine of at least €1,000. Tell family before they post or pack; big money gifts travel better by bank transfer. — douane.nl, verified 4 Oct

Deep Dive — “The dollar squeeze”

Brent Johnson’s Milkshake podcast likens world finance to a poker game in which rising interest rates are the blinds. The US is the big stack, because it prints the chips; most other countries are short stacks, at risk of being blinded out. They carry two burdens at once — their own currency and their offshore dollar debt, which the Bank for International Settlements puts above $100 trillion once swaps and forwards are counted — and a stronger dollar squeezes both. Over eight years, it says, the yen has lost about 45% against the dollar and the Brazilian real over 60%, driving investors and central banks into gold and US shares. China sits out: its bond market is over 95% domestically owned. One channel’s view, not advice. — Brent Johnson Milkshakes Pod, “Why rising rates are a problem for the USA” — content digest

De Kleine Khabri — Week 36

Thirty-six weeks and four days, 24 days to the due date. On Thursday, at 37 weeks, the baby counts as full-term; the lungs are finishing their maturing and most babies have settled head-down by now. From here the midwife is the first call for anything that worries you — fewer movements, losing fluid, or contractions that come regularly — and the hospital bag and car seat are best ready by the door. Elders, hedged and for interest only: De zuurstofsok (the oxygen-sock baby monitor). A busy thread splits parents: some credit the sock with catching a real problem, others say it cost them sleep. It is a consumer gadget, not a medical device; anything about a baby’s breathing belongs with the midwife or GP. reddit.com/r/Mommit Weinig uit de kolf (little milk from the pump). Many mothers write that the pump drew little while the baby fed and grew well at the breast; growth says more than millilitres.
