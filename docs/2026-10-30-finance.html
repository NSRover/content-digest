Luke Gromen argues that AI-driven white-collar job losses pose an existential threat to the $19 trillion US consumer debt market, risking severe bank credit losses, recessions, and ballooning national deficits. He predicts the Federal Reserve will ultimately be forced to print money to monetize expanding Treasury supply as traditional buyers pull back. Amid geopolitical turmoil, including the ongoing closure of the Strait of Hormuz, Gromen maintains a cautious stance, holding 30% of his liquid net worth in cash and T-bills.
