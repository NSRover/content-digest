  • Central banks, particularly in the Gulf region, have already been buying physical silver and taking delivery on the Comex, which has previously led to temporary closures of the futures exchange to halt physical delivery.
  • Silver has historically functioned as circulating money, as evidenced by historical U.S. silver certificates that were directly redeemable for physical silver, unlike modern Federal Reserve notes which are merely paper legal tender.
  • The global bond market is showing signs of distress, with Japanese Government Bond (JGB) yields rising rapidly and putting upward pressure on U.S. Treasuries and European government bonds.
  • While the tokenization of gold and silver is expected to increase to facilitate digital transactions as fiat currencies fail, investors should continue to hold physical precious metals to eliminate counterparty risk.
